About the mushroom

Bright, golden and unmistakable, with a gentle peppery, fruity note. Chanterelles are hand-picked and cleaned with care — they bruise easily and reward gentle handling.

We size-grade every lot so buyers get consistency in the box, and offer them fresh in season, traditionally brined, and frozen for year-round supply.

In the kitchen

Chanterelles love butter and a hot pan. Keep it simple — a quick sauté, a little garlic, parsley and they shine.
