A Season in the Kočani Forests

The forest keeps its own calendar, and after more than thirty years we have learned to keep it too. Every species has its window, and much of our work is simply being ready when it opens.

Spring

The year begins with morels and, around St George's Day, the dense and mealy ѓурѓовка. These are short, precious seasons — a few weeks where the pickers we have known for decades bring in the first baskets of the year.

Summer into autumn

As the weather warms, chanterelles light up the forest floor in gold, followed by the main event: porcini. Autumn also brings the carrot-orange saffron milk caps that are so loved across the Mediterranean.

We harvest what the season gives, and take no more.

That rhythm is why our range moves through the year, and why we preserve so carefully — fresh in season, then frozen and dried so the forest can reach a European kitchen in any month.
