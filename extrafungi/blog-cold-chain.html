From Forest to Europe: Our Cold Chain

The flavour of a wild mushroom starts to fade the moment it leaves the ground. Everything we have built — the buy-out stations, the modern facility, the freezers — exists to slow that clock down.

Speed at the start

Because our facility sits in the middle of the foraging region, deliveries reach us within hours. Fast intake means we can chill, quick-freeze or begin drying while the harvest is still at its best.

Controlled all the way

From there, product is packed to each customer's specification and moved in a controlled cold chain to buyers in France, Italy, Spain and Germany. Our location puts the whole EU within short, reliable reach.

It is not glamorous work, but it is the difference between wild food that tastes of the forest and wild food that does not.
