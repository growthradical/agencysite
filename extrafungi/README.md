# Extra Fungi — Brand Website

A minimalist, modern multi-page website for **Extra Fungi**, the family-owned
wild-mushroom company from Kočani, North Macedonia (founded 1988).

## Live

Published via GitHub Pages: `https://growthradical.github.io/agencysite/extrafungi/`

## Pages

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `index.html` | Home — hero, story, harvest preview, featured recipe video, export markets |
| `about.html` | Story since 1988, the facility, forest-to-table process, values, Extra Food brand |
| `products.html` | Full species range (porcini, chanterelle, saffron milk cap, St George's, morel), formats, fruits & vegetables, retail line |
| `recipes.html` | The chef Nikola Mishkovski recipe series — three embedded YouTube videos |
| `contact.html` | Contact form, company details, map |
| `styles.css` | Shared design system |
| `script.js` | Shared interactions (nav, scroll reveals, form) |

## Content & sources

Company facts (founded 1988, Kočani; organic-certified wild mushrooms; export to
France, Italy, Spain, Germany; retail brand "Extra Food"; species handled) are
drawn from public sources. The recipe videos are Extra Fungi's own YouTube
uploads:

- Porcini Risotto — `youtube.com/watch?v=qULxKFUD9s4` (the video on the site's `blog-detail.html`)
- Beef Wellington with Porcini — `youtube.com/watch?v=zBjpznHgU_0`
- Caesar Salad with Porcini — `youtube.com/watch?v=tTsXhe6vUcs`

## Design

Editorial minimalism — deep forest green, warm cream paper, amber accent;
`Fraunces` display serif with `Inter`. Photography loads from Unsplash as
progressive enhancement over designed fallbacks. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
