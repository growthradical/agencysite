Our story

A trade that began with a single village, a cooling room, and a respect for the forest.

Extra Fungi started in 1988 as a small family enterprise — buying wild forest mushrooms from the pickers of the Kočani region and processing them in our own facility. What began as a seasonal trade has grown into one of North Macedonia's trusted names in wild-food export.

Today we operate a modern cooling and processing facility built to European standards, and we have widened our baskets to include fresh fruits and vegetables from the same fertile valleys. The forest still sets our calendar — but our standards are set by the chefs and buyers who depend on us.

Forest to table

How it reaches
your kitchen.

A short, honest chain that protects the flavour of wild food — from the pickers we've known for decades to the container that leaves for Europe.

01

Foraged

Local pickers harvest by hand across the forests of eastern Macedonia, exactly in season.

02

Bought & sorted

Each delivery is weighed, inspected and graded at our buy-out station within hours of picking.

03

Processed

Cleaned, then chilled, quick-frozen or slowly dried in our facility, built to modern EU standards.

04

Exported

Packed to spec and shipped in a controlled cold chain to buyers across the European Union.
