Our story

A trade that began with one village, a cooling room, and a respect for the forest.

Extra Fungi started in 1988 as a small family enterprise — buying wild forest mushrooms from the pickers of the Kočani region and processing them in our own facility. Under the family's leadership it has grown into one of North Macedonia's trusted names in wild-food export.

Today we run a modern, full-scale cooling and processing facility built to European standards, most of our range is organic-certified, and we have widened our baskets to include orchard fruit and fresh vegetables from the same fertile valleys.

The harvest

What we bring
out of the forest.

Every basket is graded, cleaned and preserved the way each species deserves — fresh on ice, brined, quick-frozen at peak, or slowly dried to concentrate the flavour. Supplied to importers, distributors and food manufacturers.

Signature

Porcini

Vrgan' · Boletus edulis

The prize of the Balkan forest — firm, nutty and deeply aromatic. Fresh in season, IQF-frozen and sliced-and-dried year round.

Golden

Chanterelles

Lisičarka · Cantharellus cibarius

Bright, peppery and unmistakably golden. Hand-cleaned and graded by size for restaurant and retail buyers.

Seasonal

Saffron Milk Caps

Rujnica · Lactarius deliciosus

Autumn's carrot-orange delicacy, prized across the Mediterranean. Offered fresh, brined and frozen.
