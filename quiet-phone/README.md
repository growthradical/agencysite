# Green Grid, Silent Phone

Service blueprint: why local visibility reports go green while the phone stays
quiet, and the measurement system that finds out which stage actually broke.

Live: `https://growthradical.github.io/agencysite/quiet-phone/`

This is the productised form of Paper 03 on the [research board](../research-board/).
The paper asks whether geo-grid share of voice predicts revenue. This answers the
question a business owner asks instead: *mine is green, so why is the phone quiet?*

## Contents

1. **The eight stages** — impression → selection → contact → answer → qualify →
   book → show → sell. A geo-grid measures stage one and reports it as the health
   of the whole chain.
2. **The twenty-four causes** — grouped by the stage they break (measurement,
   visibility, selection, capture, intake, market). Each carries the tell that
   distinguishes it from the other twenty-three, how to confirm it, and what it costs.
3. **Measuring local honestly** — grid parameters that stop flattering, the headroom
   metric, and the three free demand sources that belong next to it.
4. **Capturing and reading the phone** — source-level tracking, transcription,
   a fourteen-field classification schema, and closing the loop to revenue.
5. **One operational dashboard** — the entity model, six named AI jobs each with a
   guardrail, and five role surfaces.
6. **Rollout** — 30/60/90 with checklists, and the honest cost shape.
7. **Consent, privacy and the things that bite** — two-party consent, health
   information, privilege, redaction, retention, and how to introduce call scoring
   to staff without destroying the front desk.

## Notes

- Field observations are anonymised to category level. No client, employer or
  prospect is named.
- All four figures are schematics of the mechanism, not measured data, and are
  captioned as such.
- Section 07 flags where regulatory risk sits so it can be raised with someone
  qualified. It is not legal advice and says so.

## Editing

One self-contained `index.html`, no dependencies, no build step. Content lives in
four arrays at the top of the `<script>` block:

- `STAGES[]` — the six cause groupings and their colours
- `CAUSES[]` — the twenty-four, each `{s, t, w, f, c, x}` (stage, title, what, tell,
  confirm, cost)
- `ROLES[]` — the five dashboard surfaces
- `PHASES{}` — the three rollout checklists

Diagrams are generated by `funnel()`, `ceiling()`, `pipeline()` and `arch()`.
Checklist state persists per reader in `localStorage`.
